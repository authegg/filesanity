## What the live sites show

1. **One rendered object as the hero.** A single physical thing, rendered in 3D or photographed like a product, alone on a staged field. Moto Finance: a metal card on a black plinth in a rock cave. Butter: a bunch of glossy 3D keyrings hanging over a pale grey field, with a big centred headline under them. Same mechanism as teenage.engineering, done glossy.
2. **Cinematic, slightly surreal photography.** One staged scene, full-bleed. Mercury: an office desk and chair standing alone on a misty mountain ridge. Milledollars: a tennis player hanging mid-air over a blue salt flat. Odyssée: a calm studio portrait. The photo is the brand. The product appears later.
3. **Serif display is back in product companies.** Granola ("The AI notepad for back-to-back meetings") and incident.io set the headline in a sharp serif over a sans UI. It reads as considered, not template.
4. **The product window under the headline.** Linear, Attio and incident.io. This is round 2, which the owner rejected. It is the category default now, so it does not differentiate.
5. **Privacy brands own a colour.** Mullvad: a saturated mid blue with yellow buttons and mono caps labels, and the app tilted on the right. Proton: soft violet. Neither looks like a generic SaaS.
6. **Dark with a lit abstract.** Raycast: red glass streaks on black. Linear: near-black. Common, and close to the Dark SaaS cluster.

Also everywhere: announcement bars, cookie banners, logo walls. FileSanity has no customers and sets no cookies, so it skips all three, and that absence is itself a trust signal.

## What fits FileSanity
- Trends 1, 2 and 3 are what separate this month's best pages from round 2.
- Trend 5 suits a privacy product.
- Trend 4 is what round 2 already was.
